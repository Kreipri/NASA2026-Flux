# 7-Week Mission: Timeline + Story Beats v1 (2026-11-15)

**Status:** D-028 (7 weeks, 1 turn = 1 week) decided by Bea. This file is the proposed timeline and beats. It replaces the 7-day beats in `storyline-v1.md` §3c. Objective: `objective-v1.md`. Facts: `fact-sources.md` (F-IDs). Storms: `real-events-deck.md`.

**Mission (in-world, D-027):** your crew of 4 is the first to live in the new base at the Connecting Ridge, for 7 weeks. Keep everyone safe and complete the 4 tasks.

---

## 1. One turn = one week

```
MONDAY BRIEF ─▶ PLAN THE WEEK ─▶ WEEK PLAYS (time-lapse) ─▶ WEEK SUMMARY
 Mission Control   spend crew time      Sun circles the sky;     cause → effect,
 (if Earth is up)  on jobs              ALERT DAYS pause it      1 "Did you know?",
 or Base Bot       + 1 crew ask         for 1 decision           task progress
```
- **Crew time:** keep the abstract 8 blocks, now per week. Jobs cost 1, 2 or 4.
- **Alert days:** max 1-2 a week, each a single choice (e.g. "Shelter now?"), resolved straight away. This is the drama, and it keeps 7-year-olds in the moment.
- **Weekly jobs follow what crews really do** (F60-F62): exercise (stamina), science (samples, instruments, radiation logs), repairs (dust, seals), moonwalks and rover trips, **call home (only when Earth is up)**, plant experiment (F62 ⚠, optional).

## 4. Week-by-week story beats

| Week | Brief (story beat) | Crew ask | Alert day(s) | Did you know? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Settle in** | Mission Control: "Welcome, first crew! Let's get the base running." Base Bot's tour runs here. | Engineer: "Panels and air first." Geologist: "Then the crater?" | Small dust event | The Sun never sets here (F07) |
| **2 First storm** | "The Sun just flared. Particles may follow in a few hours." Mission Control also forecasts: "In week 3 we lose contact. In week 4 a long shadow. Prepare." | Doctor: "Ready the shelter." | **Storm 1** hits: shelter now? | Radiation jumped ~600x in May 2024 (F30) |
| **3 Alone** | "Earth is sinking below your horizon. See you in two weeks, Commander." | Engineer: "Battery check before the dark." | Maybe **Storm 2** (alarm only) | Why Earth disappears: tidal locking + wobble (F18, F20) |
| **4 The long shadow** | Base Bot: "Three days of darkness. We run on batteries." | Geologist: "Dark crater, dark ridge... wait for light?" | Long shadow starts; maybe Storm 2 | Some craters haven't seen sunlight for 2 billion years (F09) |
| **5 Earth returns** | "We hear you! Tell us everything." Mission Control catches up. A lunar eclipse is forecast. | Doctor: check-ups and exercise (Solar max: "Can we call home?", job unlocked, D-032) | **Eclipse** (a few hours of power dip); maybe **Storm 3** | From the Moon, a lunar eclipse means Earth blocks the Sun (F39) |
| **6 Big push** | "Best light of the mission. Last good window for the crater." | Geologist: "Now or never for the crater!" | Maybe Storm 3 (false alarm?) | Ice holds clues to the solar system's past (F29) |
| **7 Final week** | "Earth sets again. The final week is yours." | Doctor: "Let's bring everyone home healthy." | 50% **Storm 4** (alarm only) | Moonquakes can last up to 10 min (F41) |

**Arc:** weeks 1-2 learn with help → weeks 3-4 alone (storm + shadow) → weeks 5-6 help returns, push for science → week 7 alone again, the final test. Each half follows the same "with help → alone" shape, so kids practise it twice.
