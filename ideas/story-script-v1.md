**Labels (right edge):** `✓F##` = NASA fact with a source card (`fact-sources.md`) · `▣` = real NASA data · `~` = game timing · `✎` = story. No new facts in this script; every ✓ already has a source.
**Cast (proposals):** You, the Commander · **Curio** (helper AI) · **Bram** (dog, engineer) · **Remy** (rabbit, doctor) · **Mica** (squirrel monkey, geologist) · **Mission Control** (voice from Earth).

---

## THE STORY IN ONE BREATH
People once visited the Moon for a few days. Now they want to stay. You lead the first crew to live in a new base at the Moon's south pole, for 7 weeks. The Sun never sets there, but storms come, a long shadow comes, and twice Earth sinks out of sight. Mission Control helps you at first. Then you're alone, and you learn to make the calls yourself. When Earth rises again, you tell them everything, and the next crew lands.

**P2** · *Same view. Now a small base with warm windows.*
> NARRATOR: Now NASA is planning a Moon base, so people can **stay**. Live there, work there, for weeks at a time. `✓F03`

**P3** · *The Moon up close. Far behind it, a tiny red Mars. A dotted line joins them.*
> NARRATOR: The Moon is our **practice ground**. What we learn there will help us reach Mars one day. `✓F04`

**P4** · *An astronaut looks back at a tiny Earth.*
> NARRATOR: The Moon is about **3 days** away by rocket. No one can pop over with more air or power. **Every choice counts.** `✓F05 ✎`

## PART 4 · THE SEVEN WEEKS

Each week: **brief** → you plan → **alert** (a choice mid-week) → **reaction** → **Did you know?**
Mission Control speaks when Earth is up. Curio speaks when Earth is down.

### Week 1 · Settle in · Earth ● up
> MISSION CONTROL: Welcome to the base, first crew! This week, get everything humming.
> MICA: Can we see the dark crater soon?
> BRAM: Base first, then rocks.
> ⚠ ALERT: Dust on the panels! Power is dropping. **[Clean now] [Later]** `✓F68`
> BRAM: *(cleaned)* Shiny panels, happy batteries. / *(not)* Those panels are getting dusty, Commander.
> DID YOU KNOW? Shadows here can be 25 to 50 times longer than the thing casting them. `✓F08`

### Week 2 · The first storm · Earth ● up
> MISSION CONTROL: Heads up! The Sun just flared. Particles may follow. And plan ahead: in week 3 we lose contact, and in week 4 a long shadow comes. `▣ ~`
> REMY: Let's make sure the shelter is ready.
> ⚠ ALERT: Mission Control: Radiation is rising. Bring everyone in? **[Shelter now] [Finish the job]** `▣`
> REMY: *(sheltered)* Doses stayed low. Good call. / *(not)* That was a lot of radiation. Next time, let's come in sooner.
> DID YOU KNOW? *(this storm's fact, e.g.)* In May 2024, radiation around the Moon jumped about 600 times in a few hours. `▣F30`

### Week 3 · Alone · Earth ○ down
> MISSION CONTROL *(static)*: Earth is sinking below your horizon. We'll hear from you in two weeks. You're in charge, Commander.
> CURIO: No more warnings from Earth. If a storm comes, our own **alarm** will tell us.
> BRAM: Let's fill the batteries before the dark week.
> ⚠ ALERT *(maybe)*: 🔔 Our alarm says radiation is rising. No one on Earth can check. Your call. **[Shelter now] [Keep working]** `▣`
> BRAM: *(batteries full)* Batteries topped up. Bring on the dark.
> DID YOU KNOW? The Moon always shows Earth the same face. But it wobbles a little, so from here Earth bobs up and down, and sometimes dips out of sight. `✓F18 F20`

### Week 4 · The long shadow · Earth ○ down
> CURIO: The ridge is heading into shadow for about 3 days. We run on **batteries** now. `~`
> MICA: It's dark out there anyway. Crater trip, or wait for the light?
> ⚠ ALERT: The last sunlight just left the panels. **[Save power] [Keep everything on]**
> BRAM: *(power never hit zero)* We made it through the dark without a blackout!
> DID YOU KNOW? Some craters near here haven't seen sunlight for up to 2 billion years. `✓F09`

### Week 5 · Earth returns · Earth ● up
> MISSION CONTROL: Commander, we hear you again! Tell us everything. And get ready: this week Earth passes in front of the Sun. `✓F39`
> REMY: Can we call home? Everyone misses their families. *(Call home unlocked)* `✓F61`
> ⚠ ALERT: Earth is blocking the Sun! Power will dip for a few hours. **[Save power] [Carry on]** `✓F39`
> ⚠ ALERT *(maybe)*: The Sun just flared, a big one! **[Shelter now] [Keep working]** `▣`
> *(if no particles came)* CURIO: No particles came this time. Not every flare sends radiation our way.
> MICA: *(kept working)* We kept working, and the data says that was right!
> DID YOU KNOW? *(false alarm)* A big flare doesn't always send radiation to the Moon. In February 2024, one didn't. `▣F38` / *(otherwise)* From the Moon, Earth has phases too! `✓F17`

### Week 6 · The big push · Earth ● up
> MISSION CONTROL: Best light of the whole mission. If you want the crater, **this is the week**. `~`
> MICA: Now or never, Commander!
> ⚠ ALERT *(on a crater trip)*: MICA: It's freezing down here. Grab one more sample, or head back to the light? **[One more] [Head back]**
> MICA: *(sample)* Look at this! Is there ice in it? Let's find out. / *(no trip)* Maybe the next crew will reach it.
> DID YOU KNOW? Ice may hold clues to how the Moon and our solar system formed. `✓F29`

### Week 7 · The final week · Earth ○ down
> MISSION CONTROL *(static)*: Earth is setting again. The final week is yours. We believe in you.
> REMY: Let's bring everyone home healthy.
> ⚠ ALERT *(50%)*: 🔔 CURIO: Radiation alarm. This one's all you. **[Shelter now] [Keep working]** `▣`
> REMY: *(crew healthy)* Seven weeks, and everyone's healthy. That's the best result there is.
> DID YOU KNOW? Moonquakes can make the ground shake for up to 10 minutes. `✓F41`
> **[ Finish mission ▶ ]**

## PART 5 · THE ENDING

### 5a. Earth rises
*Time-lapse: the Sun circles once more, fast. "1 week later." Earth rises over the horizon. The radio crackles.* `✓F20 ~`
> MISSION CONTROL: First crew, we hear you! **Tell us everything!**

### 5b. Your story *(built from what the player did; 2-3 of these)*
> CURIO: Here's your story:
> 🛡 You sheltered during a **real** solar storm from [May 10, 2024]. `▣`
> 🔔 When Earth was gone and the alarm went off, **you** made the call.
> 🙂 You didn't panic at the false alarm. You checked, and kept working. `▣`
> 🔋 Your batteries lasted the long shadow.
> 🛸 You mapped the crater with the drone before driving in.
> 🧊 You brought back samples from a crater colder than Pluto. `✓F09`

### 5c. The crew says goodbye *(one line each, picked by how it went; always one kind thing)*
> BRAM: Not one blackout. Nice planning! / That dark stretch was close. Charge up earlier next time.
> REMY: Lowest radiation dose I've seen! / We took a lot of radiation in week [N]. Next time, let's come in sooner.
> MICA: Apollo rocks are still studied 50 years later. Ours will keep scientists busy too! `✓F66` / Next time, let's make it to the crater.

### 5d. The stamp
> **GOLD PATCH** / **HONORS** · That's real astronaut thinking.
> **MISSION COMPLETE** · Crew safe, tasks done. The base works!
> **NOT YET** · Real missions come back with "not yet" too. Here's what to try next: [tip]. *(+ one thing you did well)*

### 5e. The next crew lands
*A lander's light drifts down near the base. Our crew waves from the doorway.*
> CURIO: The base worked. Now the next crew can live here too.
> BRAM: I left them a note. It says: **clean the panels.** `✎`

### 5f. What you learned
*The ridge in its ring of sunlight. Then the base. Then the dark crater.*
> CURIO: Here, **sunlight is power**. Where you go decides your power, how cold it gets, and how much radiation you take. `✎`
> CURIO: Real crews will make these choices one day. Maybe one of them will be **you**.

### 5g. Next mission
*A dusty orange Mars. A dimmer Sun.*
> MISSION 2: **MARS** 🔒 Weaker sunlight, dust storms, and messages that take up to 20 minutes. `✓F50 F51 F52`

*Then the debrief: your 7 weeks in charts, the real storms you met, and Play again / Solar max / How real is this?*

### Other ending paths
**Not yet** (all 7 weeks done, crew safe, tasks short): same as above, the NOT YET stamp, and in 5e Curio says: "The base kept you safe. The next crew will pick up where you left off."

**Crew called home** (a meter failed before week 7):
*No stamp. The crew boards the lander. The base lights dim.*
> CURIO: [Power] got too low to stay safe, so the crew headed home early. Everyone is safe. That's what the emergency plan is for. `✎`
> REMY: Everyone's okay. That's what matters.
> CURIO: Here's your tip: [Fill the batteries before the long shadow.] Want to try again?
*Then 5f, 5g and the debrief.*

**Tips by cause:** power ran out → Fill the batteries before the long shadow. · high dose → When the alarm sounds, go to the shelter first. `✓F67` · tired crew → Plan rest and exercise, not just work. · no ice evidence → Send the drone first, then the rover in good light.

## What changed from storyline-v2 (for review)
1. **The briefing is now spoken by Mission Control, not Curio.** Curio isn't introduced until M4, and Earth is up at the start. Mission Control "gives" the mission; Curio takes over in 3D.
2. **The prelude has a narrator** (on-screen text; voice later is a stretch goal).
3. **P4 is shorter:** "No one can pop over with more air or power."
4. **New lines:** Bram's ending note ("clean the panels", a callback to week 1), Remy in the called-home path, and Curio's "Not yet" next-crew line. All are story lines with no new facts.
5. **Lesson reworded** for kids: "how cold it gets" and "how much radiation you take" replace "cold and radiation risk".
6. **Called-home ending written out:** the crew takes the lander home early. Everyone is safe and there's no stamp.
