# Title Drafts v1 (2026-11-15)

**Status:** proposed. Working title was "Mission: Outpost". The subtitle stays **"A Junior Astronaut Mission"** (it ties the game to the hackathon brief). The title screen art is still "Lamp on the Ridge" (`title-screen-drafts-v1.md` A).

**What a good title does here:** a 7-year-old can read and say it, it tells you who you are or where you go, it still works for Mission 2 (Mars), and it doesn't use NASA or Artemis branding.

| # | Title | Works for Mars? | Upside | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **First Crew** · Moon Base ⭐ | "First Crew · Mars" | Says who you are and matches the story ("first crew!") and the ending. Easy words | A bit plain |
| 2 | **The Sun That Never Sets** | No | Hooks the key fact and the lesson | Long, and Moon-only |
| 3 | **Moon Base Commander** | "Mars Base Commander" | Kids love "commander"; it's what you are | Generic; other games may already use it |
| 4 | **Ridge Crew** | Weak | Short and catchy, sounds like a team | Vague: "ridge" is a hard word |
| 5 | **Lamp on the Ridge** | No | Pretty, and matches the title art | Kids won't know what it means |
| 6 | **Space Commander** (Bea) | Yes | Kids love it; it's what you are; works for any planet | **Taken:** "Space Commander: War and Trade" is on Steam and Switch, and there are several other games with the name. Hard to find in a search. It also sounds like a battle game |
| 7 | **Space Base Commander** (Bea) | Yes: "Mission 2: Mars" | Kids love "commander"; says you run a base; works for any planet; easy words | No exact match found (2026-11-15). Close: "Halcyon 6: Starbase Commander" (a Steam strategy game) and "Space Base" (a dice board game). A bit long; "Space" is vaguer than "Moon" |

**Update:** Bea's #7 is a strong pick. The name is free and exciting. Suggested lockup: **Space Base Commander · Mission 1: The Moon**, with "A Junior Astronaut Mission" as the small tag. Keep "first crew" in the story lines.

**Earlier recommendation: #1, "First Crew: Moon Base · A Junior Astronaut Mission".** Mission Control says "first crew" all game, so the title pays off at the end. It also turns into a series: First Crew: Mars.
**If we want "Commander" in it:** use it in the tagline: "First Crew: Moon Base · You're the Commander". Or use a more specific title: "Moon Base Commander" (not yet checked for clashes).
**Before locking:** do a quick name search (app stores, web) for clashes.
