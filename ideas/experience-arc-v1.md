# Experience Arc v1 (proposed, Bea's direction 2026-11-14)

Story intro -> hands-on 7-day mission -> ending story with praise -> optional replay. Fun facts at the right moments, each with a source line.

## 1. Opening story (skippable, 4-5 cards, 1-2 sentences each, ~60 s)
1. "One day, people will live on the Moon. Before they do, someone has to find a safe place to build."
2. "That's you. You and your crew of 4 are at a real place: a ridge near the Moon's south pole." `[✓ NASA]`
3. "Up here the Sun almost never sets. Sunlight is your power." / "Down in the dark craters it's colder than Pluto, but there might be ice." `[✓ NASA]`
4. "The Sun can also throw storms of radiation. You'll need to know when to hide." `[✓ NASA]`
5. "You have 7 days to prove this ridge can be a Moon base. Every choice costs something. Ready, Commander?"

Teaches *why it matters*. Rules are taught on day 1 by doing, not here.

## 2. Hands-on mission
The existing 7-day mission (D-020/D-021). No change.

## 3. Ending story (every tier gets one)
- Praise names **the player's own choices**: "You moved your crew into the shelter during a real solar storm from May 2024."
- GOLD / APPROVED: "NASA-style planners would say this ridge is ready."
- PROMISING: "Strong start. One more trip and this site is proven."
- NOT YET: "Real missions come back with 'not yet' too. Here's what to try next." Still praise one thing they did well.
- Last card: the lesson in one line: "Light is a resource. Where you work decides your power, cold, and radiation risk."

## 4. Replay (optional)
`[Play again: new surprises]` `[Harder: 14 days]` `[I'm done]`. Replay varies via seed + surprise storm.

## 5. Fun facts
- Triggered by what just happened (storm -> storm fact, crater trip -> cold fact), max 1 per day, shown in the end-of-day summary.
- Draft set (all already verified, see `research-setting-facts.md`):
  - Shackleton Crater is 21 km wide and 4 km deep. The south pole sits on its rim. `NASA`
  - Shadowed craters can drop to -414 F, colder than Pluto. `NASA`
  - In the May 2024 storm, radiation in lunar orbit jumped about 600x in hours. `NASA RadLab (CRaTER), our arithmetic`
  - A south pole base can see Earth only about half of each month. `NASA/JPL study`
  - The ISS recycles about 98% of its water. `NASA`
