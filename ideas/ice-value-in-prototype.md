# Ice in the current prototype

**Correction after NASA source check:** the description below records the implemented mechanic, not a scientifically supported benefit. The earlier recommendation to retain the permanent discount is withdrawn. See `ice-science-check.md` for evidence and replacement options.

Clarification, 2026-11-14; no design change.

- Ice is an investment: each of the first two samples permanently reduces the power cost of Tune air by 6. Cost goes 20 -> 14 -> 8; oxygen gained stays +30 per use. Samples are not consumed.
- Ice contributes report-card evidence: a trip earns 1 star, one find earns 2, two finds earn 3. It is not required to survive.
- A rover trip costs 50 power, 8 oxygen, 4 crew time, outside exposure, and adds dust. Base success is 30%; scouting costs another 15 power and 1 crew time for +25 percentage points on the next trip (cap 80%). All are simulated game numbers.
- Its design role is to tempt the player away from the powered base for a possible future benefit. Late finds have less time to repay their cost.
- D-013 mentions water as well, but the engine currently implements only the air-system power discount and report-card evidence. No separate water supply or ice-processing job exists.

Recommendation: retain the role and make the benefit explicit in the player-facing explanation. Small samples granting a permanent discount are a game abstraction, not a physical extraction model. No change approved or implemented.
