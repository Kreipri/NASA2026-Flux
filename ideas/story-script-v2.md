# Story Script v2: simpler words, more showing (2026-11-15)

**Status:** for Bea to finalize. Replaces `story-script-v1.md`. Changes: simpler words, pictures do more of the work, tricky words are tappable, Curio and Mission Control have separate jobs (D-031, proposed), and the ending matches NASA's plan that the base stays on the Moon (F69).
**Labels:** `✓F##` = NASA fact with a source card (`fact-sources.md`) · `▣` = real NASA data · `~` = game timing · `✎` = story · `📖` = tappable word (glossary at the bottom).

---

## 0. Writing rules (ages 7-12)
1. **If the picture can show it, cut the words.** Every card starts with a **SHOW** line. The text only says what the picture can't.
2. **Short:** max 12 words per sentence and 2 sentences per card.
3. **One idea per sentence.** No "which…", "because…", "so that…" chains.
4. **Everyday words.** If a hard word is needed, give it a dotted underline. Tap it on a tablet or hover on a computer to get a 1-line meaning and a small picture.
5. **Numbers become pictures:** a counter (12 people), a calendar flipping (3 days), a bar shooting up (600x).

## 1. Who talks when (D-031, proposed)

**Mission Control = the boss on Earth.** This is their real job:
| What they really do | Source | In the game |
|---|---|---|
| Watch the base all day and night | ✓F70 | Warnings: "Your power is low" (only when Earth is up) |
| Plan the crew's days | ✓F71 | The **Monday brief**: this week's plan and goal |
| Watch the Sun for radiation storms | ✓F67 | Storm warnings, before the storm hits |
| Talk to the crew (the "capcom" does this) | ✓F70 | One voice from Earth |
| Make the big calls | ✓F70 | Gives you the mission. Calls the crew home if it's unsafe |

**Curio = your helper in the base.** Curio fills the gaps Mission Control can't:
| Gap | Why there's a gap | Curio's job |
|---|---|---|
| Earth is gone for weeks 3-4 and 7 | No radio when Earth is below the horizon ✓F20 | Reads the onboard alarm. Plays "the plan Mission Control left us" |
| A kid's first time | Mission Control doesn't run tutorials | Intro, tour, first-week pointer |
| "Why?" | Mission Control gives the plan, not the reasons | Ask Curio, word pop-ups, "Did you know?" |
| "What just happened?" | | End-of-week summary, "Your story" at the end |

- **Curio suggests. You decide.** Curio never gives orders and never repeats what Mission Control just said.
- **Real basis:** NASA plans base equipment that runs on its own and works with robot helpers ✓F72. A real talking AI helper, CIMON, has flown on the space station ✓F73.

## PART 4 · THE SEVEN WEEKS

Each week: **SHOW** → **brief** → you plan → **alert** (one choice) → **reaction** → **Did you know?** (shown as a picture). Earth up = Mission Control briefs. Earth down = Curio plays the plan Mission Control left.

### Week 1 · Settle in · 🌍 up
- SHOW: Earth in the sky. Base lights click on one by one.
- MISSION CONTROL: Welcome, first crew! This week: get the base running.
- MICA: Can we visit the dark crater soon? · BRAM: Base first. Rocks later.
- ⚠ SHOW: dust creeps over a panel; the power bar dips. "Dust on the panels!" **[Clean now] [Later]** `✓F68`
- BRAM: Shiny panels, happy batteries! / Those panels are getting dusty.
- DID YOU KNOW? SHOW a pebble with a super long shadow. "Shadows here can be 50 times longer than the thing making them." `✓F08`

### Week 2 · First storm · 🌍 up
- SHOW: The Sun flashes. The radio blinks.
- MISSION CONTROL: Heads up! The Sun just made a big 📖flare. A 📖radiation storm may come. `▣`
- MISSION CONTROL: And plan ahead. Next week, we lose contact. *(SHOW: calendar, week 3 🌍✕, week 4 🌑)* `~`
- REMY: Let's get the shelter ready.
- ⚠ SHOW: the radiation bar climbs; tiny dots stream from the Sun. MISSION CONTROL: "Radiation is rising! Bring everyone in?" **[Shelter now] [Finish the job]** `▣`
- REMY: Our 📖dose stayed low. Good call! / That was a lot of radiation. Next time, come in sooner.
- DID YOU KNOW? SHOW the real data line shooting up. "In May 2024, radiation near the Moon jumped 600 times!" `▣F30`

### Week 3 · Alone · 🌍 down
- SHOW: Earth sinks below the horizon. Static. The Earth icon greys out.
- MISSION CONTROL *(crackly)*: Earth is setting in your sky. Talk in two weeks. You're in charge!
- CURIO: Now our own alarm watches for storms. I'll help you read it.
- BRAM: Let's fill the batteries before the dark week.
- ⚠ *(maybe)* SHOW: a red alarm light spins. CURIO: "Our alarm says radiation is rising. Earth can't check. Your call!" **[Shelter now] [Keep working]** `▣`
- BRAM: Batteries full. Bring on the dark!
- DID YOU KNOW? SHOW the Moon wobbling and Earth bobbing in the sky. "The Moon always shows Earth the same side. But it wobbles a bit. So from here, Earth bobs up and down." `✓F18 F20`

### Week 4 · The long shadow · 🌍 down
- SHOW: The Sun dips behind far hills. The ridge goes dark. The battery icon glows.
- CURIO: Mission Control's plan said this: 3 dark days. We use battery power now. `~`
- MICA: It's dark anyway. Crater trip? Or wait for light?
- ⚠ SHOW: the last sunbeam slides off the panels. "The sunlight is gone." **[Save power] [Keep everything on]**
- BRAM: No blackout. We made it!
- DID YOU KNOW? "Some craters here haven't seen sunlight for 2 billion years!" `✓F09`

### Week 5 · Earth returns · 🌍 up
- SHOW: Earth rises. The radio crackles. The crew cheers.
- MISSION CONTROL: We hear you again! Tell us everything.
- MISSION CONTROL: This week, Earth will block the Sun for a few hours. *(SHOW: forecast icon)* `✓F39`
- REMY: Earth's back! Time for check-ups. And exercise! `✓F60`
- ⚠ SHOW: Earth slides over the Sun; the panels dim. "📖Eclipse! Power will dip for a few hours." **[Save power] [Carry on]** `✓F39`
- ⚠ *(maybe, a small storm)* MISSION CONTROL: "Small flare. A weak storm may come. Bring everyone in?" **[Shelter now] [Keep working]** `▣`
- BRAM: Eclipse over. Power's back!
- DID YOU KNOW? SHOW Earth's phases from the Moon. "From the Moon, Earth has phases too!" `✓F17`
- **Solar max only (harder mode, D-032):**
  - REMY: Can we call home? We miss our families. *(Call home unlocked)* `✓F61`
  - ⚠ *(maybe)* MISSION CONTROL: "Big flare on the Sun!" **[Shelter now] [Keep working]** → if no storm comes, CURIO: "No storm came. Not every flare sends radiation to us." `▣F38`
  - MICA: We kept working. Good thinking!
  - DID YOU KNOW? "In February 2024, a giant flare sent no radiation storm to the Moon." `▣F38`

### Week 6 · The big push · 🌍 up
- SHOW: Bright light. The crater pin glows.
- MISSION CONTROL: Best light of the whole mission. Want the crater? Go this week! `~`
- MICA: Now or never, Commander!
- ⚠ *(on a crater trip)* SHOW: the thermometer drops; the rover's lights come on. MICA: "Brrr! One more 📖sample, or head back?" **[One more] [Head back]**
- MICA: Look! Is there ice in it? Let's find out! / Maybe the next crew will get there.
- DID YOU KNOW? "Ice may hold clues about how our solar system began." `✓F29`

### Week 7 · The final week · 🌍 down
- SHOW: Earth sets again.
- MISSION CONTROL *(crackly)*: Earth is setting again. This last week is all yours. We believe in you!
- REMY: Let's get everyone home healthy.
- ⚠ *(50%)* SHOW: the alarm light spins. CURIO: "Radiation alarm! This one's all you." **[Shelter now] [Keep working]** `▣`
- REMY: 7 weeks, and everyone is healthy. Best result ever!
- DID YOU KNOW? SHOW the ground shaking. "Moonquakes can last up to 10 minutes!" `✓F41`

## PART 5 · THE ENDING

**5a. Earth comes back** · SHOW: time-lapse, the Sun circles fast. "1 week later." Earth rises. The radio crackles. `✓F20 ~`
> MISSION CONTROL: First crew! We hear you. Tell us everything!

**5b. Your story** · SHOW: 2-3 picture cards, one at a time, picked from what you did.
> CURIO: Here's your story!
- 🛡 You hid from a **real** solar storm. It happened on May 10, 2024. `▣`
- 🔔 Earth was gone. The alarm rang. **You** made the call.
- 🙂 *(Solar max only)* A big flare, but no storm. You stayed calm. `▣`
- 🔋 Your batteries lasted through the dark.
- 🛸 You sent the drone first. Smart!
- 🧊 You brought back rocks from a crater colder than Pluto. `✓F09`

**5c. The crew** · SHOW: three portraits; speech bubbles pop in turn. Each one always says something kind.
- BRAM: No blackouts. Nice planning! / The dark was close. Charge up sooner next time.
- REMY: Lowest radiation dose I've seen! / Week [N] had lots of radiation. Come in sooner next time.
- MICA: Scientists still study Apollo rocks today. They'll study ours too! `✓F66` / Next time, let's reach the crater.

**5d. The stamp** · SHOW: the stamp thumps down in a puff of dust.
- GOLD PATCH / HONORS: You think like a real astronaut!
- MISSION COMPLETE: Crew safe. Tasks done. The base works!
- NOT YET: Real missions say "not yet" too. Try this next: [tip]. *(+ one thing you did well)*

**5e. Home, and the next crew** · *(the base stays on the Moon, ✓F69)*
- SHOW: The crew climbs into the lander. Lift-off. Below, the base lights stay on.
- CURIO *(alone at the console)*: I'll keep the base running until the next crew comes. `✓F72 ✎`
- SHOW: "Later…" A new lander touches down. The new crew finds a note on the console, in Bram's writing: **"Clean the panels! – Bram"** `✎`
- CURIO: Welcome, new crew! The base is ready.

**5f. What you learned** · SHOW: the camera moves from the sunny ridge into the dark crater. Three icons change as it goes: ⚡ power down, ❄ cold up, ☢ radiation risk.
> CURIO: Here, sunlight is power. Where you go changes everything. `✎`
> CURIO: One day, real crews will make these choices. Maybe you!

**5g. Next mission** · SHOW: a dusty orange Mars. The Sun looks smaller.
> NEXT: MARS 🔒 Weaker sunlight. Big dust storms. Messages take up to 20 minutes. `✓F50 F51 F52`

*Then the debrief: your 7 weeks as charts, the real storms you met, and Play again · Solar max · How real is this?*

### Other endings
- **Not yet** (7 weeks done, crew safe, tasks short): the NOT YET stamp. In 5e, CURIO says: "The base kept you safe. The next crew will finish the job."
- **Crew called home** (air ran out or the radiation dose got too high before week 7). Power at zero is **not** an ending: the base switches to power-saving mode (D-032). No stamp, and no one is ever hurt.
  - SHOW: a meter flashes red.
  - *(Earth up)* MISSION CONTROL: Commander, we're bringing you home. Safety first. `✎`
  - *(Earth down)* CURIO: [Air is too low / Radiation is too high] to stay safe. Time for the safety plan. `✎`
  - SHOW: the crew boards the lander. The base lights dim but stay on.
  - REMY: Everyone's okay. That's what matters.
  - CURIO: Here's a tip: [tip]. Want to try again?
  - Then 5f, 5g and the debrief.
- **Tips:** Fill the batteries before the dark. · Keep the air machine fixed. · Alarm? Go to the shelter first. `✓F67` · Plan rest, not just work. · Send the drone first, then the rover.
