**Cast (names and species are still proposals, v3):** **You**, the Commander (human) · **Curio**, the helper AI on the console · **Bram** (dog, trained as an engineer, base systems) · **Remy** (rabbit, trained as a doctor, crew health) · **Mica** (squirrel monkey, trained as a geologist, exploring).

**Labels:** `✓ F##` = NASA fact with a source card · `▣ DATA` = real NASA data, ARCHIVE, real date shown · `~` = game number or timing · `✎` = story. Every `✓` must exist in `fact-sources.md`.

**Reading rules (ages 7-12):** ≤25 words per card or line. One bold keyword per card. Per week: 1 brief + 1 crew ask + 1 reaction + max 1 "Did you know?". Alerts are 1 line + 2 buttons.

**One-sentence story:** *The first crew moves into a new Moon base for 7 weeks. The Sun never sets, but storms come, the dark comes, and twice Earth disappears, so you learn to make the calls yourself.*

## 4. The 7 weeks: beats and lines

Format per week: **Brief** (Monday) · **Ask** (one crew request, maps to a job) · **Alert** (mid-week, if any) · **Reaction** (end of week; pick by what happened) · **Did you know?**
Who speaks the brief: **Mission Control when Earth is up**, **Curio when Earth is down.**

### Week 1: Settle in (Earth up)
- **Brief (Mission Control):** "Welcome to the base, first crew! This week: get everything humming."
- **Ask:** Mica: "Can we see the dark crater soon?" / Bram: "Base first, then rocks."
- **Alert (~ dust event):** "Dust on the panels! Power is dropping." [Clean now] [Later] (why: ✓ F68)
- **Reaction:** Bram (if cleaned): "Shiny panels, happy batteries." / (if not): "Those panels are getting dusty, Commander."
- **Did you know?** "Shadows here can be 25 to 50 times longer than the thing casting them." ✓ F08

### Week 2: First storm (Earth up)
- **Brief (Mission Control):** "Heads up! The Sun just flared. Particles may follow. Also: in week 3 we lose contact, and in week 4 a long shadow comes. **Plan ahead.**"
- **Ask:** Remy: "Let's make sure the shelter is ready."
- **Alert (Storm 1, ▣ DATA):** "Mission Control: radiation is rising. Bring everyone in?" [Shelter now] [Finish the job]
- **Reaction:** Remy (sheltered): "Doses stayed low. Good call." / (didn't): "That was a lot of radiation. Next time, let's come in sooner."
- **Did you know?** The storm's own fact, e.g. "In May 2024, radiation around the Moon jumped about 600 times in a few hours." ▣ F30

### Week 3: Alone (Earth down)
- **Brief (Mission Control, last message):** "Earth is sinking below your horizon. We'll hear from you in two weeks. You're in charge, Commander."
- **Brief (Curio, after):** "No more warnings from Earth. If a storm comes, our own **alarm** will tell us."
- **Ask:** Bram: "Let's fill the batteries before the dark week."
- **Alert (maybe Storm 2):** *alarm sound* Curio: "Our alarm says radiation is rising. No one on Earth can check. Your call." [Shelter now] [Keep working]
- **Reaction:** Bram (batteries full): "Batteries topped up. Bring on the dark."
- **Did you know?** "The Moon always shows Earth the same face. It wobbles a little, so from here Earth bobs up and down, and sometimes dips out of sight." ✓ F18 F20

### Week 4: The long shadow (Earth down)
- **Brief (Curio):** "The ridge is heading into shadow for about 3 days. We run on **batteries** now."
- **Ask:** Mica: "It's dark out there anyway... crater trip, or wait for the light?"
- **Alert (~ shadow starts):** "The last sunlight just left the panels." [Switch to power-saving] [Keep everything on]
- **Reaction:** Bram (power never hit zero): "We made it through the dark without a blackout!"
- **Did you know?** "Some craters near here haven't seen sunlight for up to 2 billion years." ✓ F09

### Week 5: Earth returns (Earth up)
- **Brief (Mission Control):** "Commander, we hear you again! Tell us everything. Also: Earth will pass in front of the Sun this week, so get ready for a short dark spell."
- **Ask:** Remy: "Can we call home? Everyone misses their families." (Call home unlocked) ✓ F61
- **Alert (eclipse, ✓ F39):** "Earth is blocking the Sun! Power will dip for a few hours." [Save power] [Carry on]
- **Alert (maybe Storm 3):** "The Sun just flared, a big one!" → (if false alarm) at the end of the week: "No particles came this time. Not every flare sends radiation our way."
- **Reaction:** Mica (if the false alarm came and you didn't overreact): "We kept working, and the data says that was right!"
- **Did you know?** "A big flare doesn't always send radiation to the Moon. In February 2024, one didn't." ▣ F38 (only if the false alarm happened; otherwise F17: "From the Moon, Earth has phases too!")

### Week 6: The big push (Earth up)
- **Brief (Mission Control):** "Best light of the whole mission. If you want the crater, **this is the week**."
- **Ask:** Mica: "Now or never for the crater, Commander!"
- **Alert (~ rover):** Mica (on a crater trip): "It's freezing down here. Grab one more sample, or head back to the light?" [One more] [Head back]
- **Reaction:** Mica (sample): "Look at this! Is there ice in it? Let's find out." / (no trip): "Maybe the next crew will reach it."
- **Did you know?** "Ice may hold clues to how the Moon and our solar system formed." ✓ F29

### Week 7: The final week (Earth down)
- **Brief (Mission Control, last message):** "Earth is setting again. The final week is yours. We believe in you."
- **Ask:** Remy: "Let's bring everyone home healthy."
- **Alert (50%, Storm 4):** *alarm* Curio: "Radiation alarm. This one's all you." [Shelter now] [Keep working]
- **Reaction:** Remy (crew healthy): "Seven weeks, and everyone's healthy. That's the best result there is."
- **Did you know?** "Moonquakes can make the ground shake for up to 10 minutes." ✓ F41

### Storm alert templates (any storm from the deck)
- **Earth up:** "Mission Control: The Sun just flared, class [X]. Particles may arrive in a few hours." → "Radiation is rising. Bring everyone in?"
- **Earth down:** *alarm* "Our alarm says radiation is rising. No one on Earth can check. Your call."
- **Source card (on tap):** "This is a real solar storm from **[date]**. Flare and particle times from NASA's DONKI. Radiation measured by the CRaTER instrument near the Moon. In this game, real storms happen closer together than in real life." ▣

- "When Earth was gone and the alarm went off, **you** made the call."
- "You didn't panic at the false alarm. You checked, and kept working." ▣

- "You mapped the crater with the drone before driving in."
- "You brought back samples from a crater colder than Pluto." ✓ F09

**E3 crew lines (honest, never mean):**
- Bram: "Not one blackout. Nice planning!" / "That dark stretch was close. Charge up earlier next time."
- Remy: "Lowest radiation dose I've seen!" / "We took a lot of radiation in week [N]. Next time, let's come in sooner."
- Mica: "Apollo samples are still studied 50 years later. Ours will keep scientists busy too!" (✓ F66) / "Next time, let's make it to the crater."

**E4 tiers:**
- GOLD PATCH / HONORS: "That's real astronaut thinking."
- MISSION COMPLETE: "Crew safe, tasks done. The base works!"
- NOT YET: "Real missions come back with 'not yet' too. Here's what to try next: [tip]." + one thing done well.
- **Crew called home** (a meter failed): "Mission Control brought everyone home safely. Every crew learns something. Here's your tip: [cause-based tip]." No one is ever hurt.

**Tips by cause (examples):** power ran out → "Fill the batteries before the long shadow." · high dose → "When the alarm sounds, go to the shelter first." (✓ F67) · tired crew → "Plan rest and exercise, not just work." · no ice evidence → "Send the drone first, then the rover in good light."
